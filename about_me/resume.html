  • Maturity findings helped leadership decide to keep QA separate from Development; contributed to analysis of team size, geographic distribution, backlog health, and delivery performance behind global team-design guidance.

  • Designed role-specific capability programs, internal certifications, Communities of Practice, workshops, and onboarding; hosted more than 72 multi-day events reaching approximately 1,300 attendees and trained more than 250 Product Managers and Product Owners.
  • Lead contributor to Product Management and Business Analyst job-family definitions.
  • Taught Product Managers and Product Owners prioritization methods including Kano, Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF), and MoSCoW, plus a riskiest-failure-mode-first approach to proofs of concept.
  • Taught Sum of Squares estimation, which sizes a backlog of Epics or Features from ranged estimates (a 50%-certainty best guess and a 90%-certainty near-worst case) instead of single-point estimates, which are less reliable and overly optimistic.
  • Led the workshop “Agile Portfolio Estimation; Buffering with Risk and Dependency Analysis” at AgileCamp Silicon Valley 2017, presenting a method for breaking down major releases, estimating at any level of detail, and building plans that account for team-level risk.
